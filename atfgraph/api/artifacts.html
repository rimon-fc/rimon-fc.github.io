    putBriefArtifact

    POST /api/v2/sessions/{id}/artifacts

    Requires: invoke role

    Attach one file to a session before any run exists. The request body is the raw bytes (not JSON, not base64) with Content-Type on the allow list: image/png, image/jpeg, image/webp, application/pdf, text/*, application/json, application/yaml, application/x-yaml, application/zip (415 unsupported_media_type otherwise). X-Artifact-Name is the filename (one path segment, at most 256 bytes); X-Artifact-Kind is openapi, document, screenshot, sample, reference_node or other (default other); X-Artifact-Sha256, when sent, must match the bytes (409 sha256_mismatch). Bodies over brief_artifacts.max_file_bytes (default 25 MiB) are 413 artifact_too_large; a session total over brief_artifacts.max_session_bytes (default 100 MiB) is 413 session_artifacts_quota. Only the session owner or an admin may upload; a deleted session is 410 session_deleted.

    putArtifact

    POST /api/v2/traversers/{id}/artifacts

    Requires: invoke role, worker role

    Attach a durable artifact to an authorized traverser. data_base64 must be canonical padded standard base64 of at most 524288 decoded bytes; larger payloads return 413. The owning invoker, an admin, or a worker holding an active lease on the traverser or an ancestor may write. id, sha256, size_bytes, created_at, namespace and principal are server assigned and rejected in the request.
