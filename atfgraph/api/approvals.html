    Approvals operations

    Requesting a decision from a human or another principal while a traverser waits.

    listApprovals

    GET /api/v2/approvals

    Requires: invoke role

    Approvals of the caller's principal (every principal for an admin), newest first. Filters: traverser_id (one step), root_id (every step of one execution tree), status (pending, approved, rejected or expired; 400 invalid_input otherwise), limit (default and maximum 1000). Without filters this is the whole namespace, which grows with every gate a run raises; the Studio screens pass traverser_id.
