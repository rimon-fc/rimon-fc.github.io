    createBudget

    POST /api/v2/budgets

    Requires: invoke role

    Create an admission-time budget: one metric (tokens or cost_usd from usage receipts, or runs = admitted root executions) over a rolling window (1h, 24h, 7d, 30d) for one scope. subject is the principal, agent label or label.capability the scope binds and is absent for namespace. A deny budget (the default) refuses traversers.submit with 429 budget_exceeded once consumption in the window has reached the limit; a warn budget never blocks and only adds warnings to access decisions. Namespace budgets and budgets over another principal require admin; label and target budgets require owning the label. The creating principal owns the budget; admins see every budget in the namespace, other callers only their own. Namespaces hold at most 256 budgets (422 budgets_full).

    budgetStatus

    GET /api/v2/budgets/status

    Requires: invoke role

    Every enabled budget that would apply to an admission by principal (the caller unless an admin names another; 403 otherwise) of target (label.capability; without it only principal and namespace budgets apply), each with its headroom. allowed is false when a deny budget is exceeded, exactly as traversers.submit would refuse. Warn budgets are listed but never affect allowed.

    updateBudget

    PATCH /api/v2/budgets/{id}

    Requires: invoke role

    Compare-and-set edit of name, window, limit, action and enabled. expected_revision must equal the current revision or the request fails with 409 revision_mismatch; scope, subject and metric are immutable and any other field is rejected (400 invalid_budget). Disabling a budget suspends enforcement without losing it.

    budgetUsage

    GET /api/v2/budgets/{id}/usage

    Requires: invoke role
